FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does Valugen make its own products?

Valugen is a reseller and distributor. Alongside trusted third-party brands, we offer a range under our own Valugen name. Products are sourced from authorised manufacturers and supplied to pharmacies and retailers across the UK. The manufacturer or distributor is named on every pack.

Are the products you supply approved for sale in the UK?

Yes. Every product we supply meets the UK requirements that apply to it. Our current range is food supplements, which are labelled and sold in line with UK food supplement rules.

Can I buy products directly from this website?

No. This website is for information only and nothing is sold through it. Our products are available through our pharmacy and retail partners.

Who do you supply?

We work with independent and chain pharmacies, retailers and other trade customers. If you'd like to stock our range, get in touch using the form below.

How do I become a stockist?

Send us a message using the contact form and choose "Becoming a stockist". A member of our team will get back to you with next steps and our current range.

I'm a manufacturer. Can Valugen distribute my products?

We're always interested in hearing from manufacturers of vitamins, supplements and healthcare products. Contact us with details of your range and we'll be in touch.

I have a question about a medicine or a possible side effect. Who should I contact?

For advice about a medicine, speak to your pharmacist or GP. For urgent medical help, call NHS 111 or 999 in an emergency. You can report a suspected side effect through the MHRA Yellow Card scheme.
